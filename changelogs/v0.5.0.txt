DESCENTS — version 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-29
The Fourth Layer: the Goblets of Giants, with its chains of waterfalls and
an optional mini boss. Creatures rebuilt again as soft, Spore-like bodies
with faces and personality. Weapons, offensive and defensive abilities and
many new artifacts. Sound that no longer goes silent, smoother play on slow
devices, and a Great Fault with many windows and an entrance you can find.

LAYER 4 — THE GOBLETS OF GIANTS (1900-2400 m)
- Beyond the Fourth Gate the Gate Stair winds down through the rock and opens
  high in the wall of a vast cavern (about 700 m across and 330 m high).
- Colossal goblets (about 22 per world) rise from its floor on twisted,
  root-flared trunks with glowing cyan spots. Each cup holds a shallow lake
  that spills through a notch in its rim as a waterfall, into a lower cup or
  down to the mere on the cavern floor (about 22 falls per world, 9 of them
  into lower lakes). Mist, drizzle and blue haze fill the cavern; roots and
  vines hang from the ceiling and from under the cups.
- A chain of seven or eight goblets descends in a spiral from the Gate Stair
  to the floor, rim linked to rim by walkable root bridges. Side goblets and
  cascades of small cups step down beside it.
- Creatures adapted to the goblets: rim grazers, lake hoppers, mist jellies,
  goblet waders, spray swifts, glass snails, cup crabs, goblet stalkers and
  vine gliders, glowing like the trunks.
- THE THRONE CUP: the widest side goblet holds a knee-deep lake where the
  layer's warden sleeps, an enormous old goblet stalker crowned with glowing
  crystal. It wakes when you wade into its lake or strike it, and fights with
  a stomp that sends a wave through the water, volleys of glowing orbs and
  lunging charges; below half health it is enraged. It has a health bar and
  its own drums under the music. Fighting it is optional: climb out of its
  cup and it lets you go, returns to its throne and slowly heals. When it
  falls, its reward rises from the lake: a Special Grade Gamma Lance.
- Maps, the vertical map, discoveries, the compass and the HUD know the new
  layer (goblets, the throne, cascades, the mere and the Gate Stair).

CREATURES — OVERHAUL 2
- New bodies, built like Spore creatures: a skeleton from the genome, then a
  skin of soft parts (round cones along the bones, ellipsoids for heads,
  bellies, cheeks, ears and shells) blended with metaballs into one smooth
  surface, polygonised in a worker so building a creature costs the main
  thread almost nothing.
- Faces with character: big eyes on their own bones with irises, pupils and
  a catch-light (they follow what the animal looks at, dart about and
  blink), mouths that open (muzzles, beaks, grins, mandibles, trunks,
  suckers, tusks), eye stalks, cheeks, ears, horns, crests and hooves, paws,
  claws, pads or talons.
- 18 body plans (quadruped, hexapod, strider, biped, hopper, sprawler,
  serpent, avian, bat, glider, insect, spider, centipede, crab, slug,
  floater, golem, roller) and 8 families (mammals, reptiles, birds,
  amphibians, arthropods, molluscs, medusoids, lithoseres); 60 genes now
  include where the mass sits, how round and young the face looks, mouth
  width, limb chunkiness, bounce, playfulness, tentacles and shells.
- Animation with personality: heavy or bouncy steps by temperament; idle
  moments chosen from playfulness, energy, curiosity and sociability
  (looking around, happy hops, shaking off, stretching, yawning, sitting,
  tail wagging, head tilts, sniffing); springy ears, antennae, trunks,
  crests and tentacles; medusoids pulse, slugs creep, rollers roll.
- New calls: peeps, burbles, hums and coos for amphibians, molluscs and
  medusoids.

COMBAT, WEAPONS AND ABILITIES
- Creatures have health. You can fight with the weapon in hand: the delver's
  pick everyone carries, or a weapon artifact. Hold attack to keep striking.
- Weapons: picks, blades and spears (melee), mauls that stun, chain flails
  that drag creatures in, bolt and shard casters (projectiles) and gamma
  lances (a continuous ray that heats up). Weapons can carry an element:
  ember (burns), frost (slows), spark (arcs to a second creature) or venom
  (poisons). The weapon is held in the left hand opposite the grappling arm.
- Offensive abilities: Gamma Ray (a searing ray that pierces everything in
  its path), Shockwave, Thornburst (crystal spikes burst from the ground
  ahead and pin creatures) and Stasis (freezes the creatures in front of
  you). Defensive abilities: Aegis (a shield that absorbs damage), Phase
  Step (an untouchable dash), Mend (health over time) and Repel (creatures
  around you flee).
- New passive effects: Bulwark (less damage), Might (more weapon damage),
  Haste (faster attacks), Leech (heals from damage dealt), Thorns (returns
  bites), Toughness (more health), Second Wind (more stamina), Calm
  (creatures are slower to anger) and Darksight (see further in the dark).
- Struck creatures react by temperament: prey and the timid flee and warn
  their group; hunters, the bold and parents turn on you for a while. A
  creature health bar shows the one you struck; hit markers turn red when a
  blow kills.
- Controls: left click attacks (right click or G now fires and releases the
  grappling arm, Q or the wheel reel in), T switches weapon, V picks the next
  ability. Controller: R2 attacks, R1 fires and releases the arm, R3 picks
  the next ability. Touch: a new Attack button.

ARTIFACTS
- Many more shapes (52 kinds): lanterns, eyes, mirrors, plumes, talons,
  casters, spindles, lances, mauls, flails, shardbows, horns, cages and
  censers join the earlier ones.
- A weapon goes to your hand when you pick it up (if only the pick was
  there); its properties work while it is in hand. The journal has Wield and
  Put away buttons, describes each weapon and lists the new effects.

SOUND
- The sound no longer goes silent by itself. A watchdog listens to the
  output: if it ever carries an invalid value or falls silent while it
  should not, the mixing graph is rebuilt (and, if that keeps happening, the
  audio context itself). Every value is checked before it reaches the audio
  graph, the listener never faces straight up or down, a suspended context
  is resumed by itself, and one-shot voices are capped (creature chatter
  first) so swarms and fights cannot overload slow devices.
- A quiet room tone everywhere and shorter rests between music phrases, so
  quiet places no longer sound like the sound has died.
- Combat sounds (swings, hits, shots, the gamma ray, shields, every ability)
  and the warden's roar, stomp and fall.

PERFORMANCE
- No frame cap by default anywhere, phones and tablets included (V-Sync and
  fixed caps remain in Settings).
- Creature skins are built in a worker; new chunks are uploaded to the GPU
  within a small budget per frame; bounding volumes come from the workers;
  shaders are compiled before play begins instead of on first sight; the
  map's saved data is cached. Layer 4 is meshed apart from the layers above
  and only drawn near it, so neither half is drawn when it cannot be seen.

LAYER 3 — THE GREAT FAULT
- Many natural windows: wherever a passage runs close behind the wall, the
  rock has broken through to the void (about 30-40 windows per world, from
  the plain down to the Basin), so the descent keeps opening onto the tube.
- Clear entrances: each sinkhole is now a wide crater with an open cutting
  leading down to a big dark mouth, flanked by crystal beacons, its standing
  stones crowned with glowing crystals and crystal lanterns lighting the
  trail down to the first hall.

SAVES
- Save format version 4: saves from 0.4.0 and earlier start their seed anew,
  because the world below the Fourth Gate changed. The weapon in hand, the
  aegis and the warden's fate are saved.

TESTS
- New: Layer 4 layout (goblets, lakes, falls, a walkable chain from the Gate
  Stair to the floor), the warden (it sleeps, wakes, fights, keeps to its
  cup, lets a fleeing explorer go and can fall), weapons, combat abilities
  and combat effects. The autopilot walks from the plain through the caves
  and the Gate Stair, then down the goblet chain to the floor of Layer 4.
- New browser check (npm run test:audio): the sound comes back by itself
  after NaN reaches the graph, after the graph goes silent and after the
  browser suspends the audio context.
- The controller check now covers R2 attacking and R1 firing the arm; the
  touch layout checks include the new Attack button.
- All 60 automated tests pass, as do the browser checks (interface, play
  scenario, traversal, grappling, mobile, controller and audio).
