DESCENTS — version 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-01
Mobile support (phones and tablets, landscape).

TOUCH CONTROLS
- Floating movement stick under the left thumb (appears where you touch;
  analog walking speed, push fully to run); drag anywhere on the right to look.
- Action buttons under the right thumb: Jump, grappling Arm (Release while
  attached), Crouch, Observe (hold), Lamp.
- Buttons that appear only when useful: Take (next to an artifact), Reel in and
  Pay out (while the claw is attached), Ability (with its recharge time).
- Labels follow the situation (Leap / Drop while climbing, Let go while
  hanging on the cable).
- Pause, map and journal buttons along the top; pinch to zoom the map.
- Multi-touch: move, look and press buttons at the same time.
- Touch settings: on/off/automatic, look speed, button size and opacity;
  tablets get slightly larger buttons automatically.

LANDSCAPE AND MOBILE BROWSERS
- Touch devices are detected automatically.
- Starting a game goes full screen and locks landscape where supported.
- Holding the device upright shows a "turn your device sideways" prompt and
  pauses the game; rotating back returns to the pause menu.
- Web app manifest: adding the game to the home screen opens it full screen
  in landscape. Viewport and safe-area handling for notched phones; browser
  zoom and scroll gestures are disabled during play.
- First launch on a mobile device uses lighter graphics settings (view
  distance 60%, no shadows, half particles, no antialiasing, resolution 1x).

LAYOUT
- HUD rearranged around the thumbs on touch screens (depth, clock and worn
  artifacts top left, map/journal top right, two latest notifications).
- Compact title screen, menus, settings and journal for short landscape
  screens; the journal fills the screen; panels keep their Done/Back buttons
  in reach while their contents scroll.
- Prompts, hints, the controls screen and journal texts name the touch
  buttons instead of keys.
- The grappling arm model is drawn smaller and lower on touch screens.

TESTS
- tools/mobile-test.mjs (npm run test:mobile): an emulated phone plays in
  landscape with real multi-touch input — rotate prompt, mobile defaults,
  joystick, look, two fingers at once, jump, grappling arm and reeling,
  observe, map pinch-zoom, journal, pause, settings, turning the phone while
  playing — and checks that no control overlaps another or the HUD on a
  phone, a small phone and a tablet.
