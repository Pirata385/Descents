DESCENTS — version 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-01
First playable version.

SCOPE
- The city around the Abyss, Layer 1 (The Verdant Edge), Layer 2 (The Inverted
  Forest, including the Stone Plain) and the entrance of Layer 3 (The Great
  Fault, down to the Threshold).
- One continuous world: no loading screens between layers, no teleporting.

WORLD GENERATION
- Everything is generated from a seed with seeded random streams; the same seed
  always rebuilds the same world.
- Hexagonal terrain columns over an island of about 3 km x 2.6 km, evaluated as
  stacks of solid spans so caves, overhangs, arches, galleries and cliffs are real
  geometry.
- Terrain field built from simplex fBm, ridged noise, domain warping and angular
  noise tables: rim plateau, city terraces and vales, the Layer 1 bowl with
  terraces, ravines, outcrops and spires, the Abyss Eye, gallery chambers in the
  shaft wall, the bell chamber over the Stone Plain and the Great Fault.
- Hydrology on a coarse grid: priority-flood lakes with one level per connected
  body, flow accumulation, rivers that follow the terrain downhill with falls,
  great waterfalls where rivers reach the Eye, springs and plunge pools in Layer 2.
- Caves (branching systems and through-caves), the Great Spiral ledge, gallery
  windows, deep tunnels between layers, the Long Stair, the fault ledge.
- Procedural city: radial avenues and ring streets, districts, houses, towers,
  markets with stalls and fountains, the Delvers' Guild, temple, observatory,
  windmills, hamlets and fields, gates with stairways, rim walls, observation
  platforms with cranes and telescopes.
- Ruins, arches, monoliths and crystal geodes placed by context.
- Route planner (A* on the coarse grid, smoothing, height profiles, multi-level
  stamping, bridges and decks) with walkability validation of every journey.

RENDERING
- Quadtree chunk streaming with six levels of detail, meshed in web workers.
- Terrain shader with baked exposure and ambient occlusion, depth-dependent abyss
  light, strata, hex edges, glowing crystals, fungus, lava veins and lit windows.
- Vegetation: grasses, meadows, flowers, shrubs, trees, conifers, ferns, reeds,
  crops, mushrooms, hanging vines, inverted trees, pale plain trees; wind sway.
- Water surfaces, river flow, waterfalls, sky with clouds, sun, moon and stars,
  ocean, day/night cycle, per-layer fog and light, light shafts into the Eye,
  shadows, atmospheric particles and waterfall mist.

EXPLORER
- First-person walking, running, crouching, jumping, step-up, mantling, climbing
  with stamina, swimming with river currents, fall damage, respawn at the last
  safe position.
- The Mechanical Grappling Arm: 60 m claw, physical cable constraint (swing,
  reel in, pay out, pull-up over ledges), cable snagging on edges.

CREATURES
- About 30 species per world in five families (mammals, reptilians, birds,
  arthropods, lithoseres) generated from ecological roles and genomes
  (morphology, colours, patterns, behaviour, diet, senses, social life,
  reproduction, activity period, voice).
- Skinned procedural meshes with per-individual colours and proportions, and
  procedural animation for walking, running, hopping, slithering, rolling,
  flying, gliding, ceiling crawling, grazing, calling and resting.
- Ecosystem: abstract population dynamics per region plus individual agents near
  the explorer that forage, drink, hunt, flee, defend territory, court, give
  birth, raise young, flock, live in symbiosis, scavenge, sleep and migrate.
- Creatures of Layers 1 and 2 ignore the explorer; the predator of the Great
  Fault stalks and attacks, and retreats from a bright lamp.

ARTIFACTS
- About 55 artifacts per world with procedural shapes, materials, rarity grades,
  categories, effects, abilities and drawbacks, placed in ruins, cave chambers,
  on spires, in crystal geodes, by waterfalls and at the Threshold.
- Pick up, three equipment slots, gradual identification, abilities (flare,
  updraft, echo pulse, glide, survey) and the explorer's lamp.

DISCOVERY, CATALOGS AND MAPS
- Creature catalog: recorded on sight, facts unlocked by observing (hold F),
  witnessed interactions recorded, 3D turntable preview.
- Artifact catalog with appearance, category, rarity, known properties and
  discovery location.
- Explorer's map of explored terrain only (three depth bands, hillshade,
  contours, landmarks, routes walked, artifact sites, trail, layer boundaries).
- Vertical abyss map: all layers by depth, deepest point, places, known routes
  and a true terrain cross-section along the explorer's bearing.
- Landmark, route and layer discovery with notifications and layer banners.

AUDIO
- Procedural Web Audio: wind, water, rivers, waterfalls, cave air and reverb,
  drips, the city, unseen birds and insects, footsteps per material, grappling
  arm sounds, creature calls synthesised from each species' genome, interface
  sounds and generative music per layer.

INTERFACE AND SAVES
- Title screen with seed entry, loading screen, pause menu, settings (view
  distance, resolution, particles, shadows, field of view, mouse, volumes),
  controls screen, HUD with compass, depth, health, stamina, prompts and
  notifications.
- Saves in browser storage (manual, autosave every two minutes and on reaching a
  new layer), load list, export/import as JSON files.

TESTS
- npm test: determinism, seed variety, city, layer structure, route validation
  and connectivity, water rules, caves and cliffs, species variety, food webs,
  inheritance, headless ecosystem simulation, artifact placement and effects,
  save round trips, performance budgets.
- Browser tests with Playwright for the interface, a gameplay scenario and a
  traversal test in which an autopilot walks every guaranteed route from the
  city to the Threshold using the real player physics, and a grappling test
  that climbs a rim cliff with the arm alone.
