DESCENTS — version 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-14
Sound you can hear, render distance in chunks with edge fog, an uncapped
frame rate with an optional limit, creatures that keep appearing everywhere,
and the Great Fault reshaped into a bright, pale tube with marked entrances.

SOUND
- The mix was far too quiet (around -33 dB, footsteps around -59 dB): a
  compressor, make-up gain and limiter now bring it to a normal listening
  level (about -18 dB) without clipping; footsteps, jumps, landings and
  creature calls were rebalanced and the deep hum and cave rumble lowered.
- Sound starts (or restarts after the phone was locked or another tab took
  over) on any click, tap or key press. iPhones and iPads play even with
  the silent switch on (where the browser allows it).
- If the browser is still holding sound back (for example when the game was
  started with a controller only, which browsers do not count), a notice
  says so.
- A NaN from the environment can no longer stop the picture: audio errors
  are reported once and skipped.

RENDER DISTANCE
- The whole map is no longer rendered. Like a block game, only the chunks
  within the render distance are built and drawn (Settings -> Graphics ->
  Render distance, 3 to 32 chunks of 32 m). Chunks that cross the edge are
  split small so nothing coarse pokes out beyond it.
- A fog closes in from 60% of the render distance and hides the edge
  completely; it is measured from the eye, so the edge is a circle and does
  not swing as the view turns. The low sky takes the colour of the fog, and
  the great falls and light shafts fade at the edge too.
- Presets: Low 6 chunks, Medium 9, High 12, Ultra 20. The old "view
  distance" slider is now "Terrain detail" (how far full detail reaches).

FRAME RATE
- No frame cap: "Unlimited" draws a frame on every screen refresh and more
  in between, while leaving the browser time to present them and handle
  input. "Display refresh rate (V-Sync)" and caps of 30 to 240 FPS are
  available under Settings -> Graphics -> Frame rate limit. Phones and
  tablets start in V-Sync to save battery.
- Automatic quality no longer lowers the quality because of a cap the
  player chose.

CREATURES
- Creatures keep appearing as you travel, in every layer: whenever there is
  room under the creature limit, new individuals are placed in real habitat
  around you (out of sight, in the kind of place their species lives), and
  those left behind are folded back into their region's population to make
  room. Before, a region's numbers ran down as its creatures were seen,
  killed or saved, and could stay empty.
- The creature area follows the render distance (up to ~170 m); creatures
  are drawn within it.
- In the Great Fault, the fliers and climbers of the open tube are favoured
  where the tube is in view; the cave fauna fills the caves.
- Layer 3 species wear pale greys, slate blues and soft browns (a few are
  crimson) instead of ember and soot tones.

LAYER 3 — THE GREAT FAULT
- Reshaped into a wide, nearly round tube (about 250-300 m across) that
  drops almost sheer for a kilometre, with gentle ledges and overhangs and
  caves inside its walls. The mouth in the Stone Plain is the top of the
  tube.
- Pale limestone and granite walls with bedding layers and speckle; moss
  and grass tufts on the open ledges; pale stalagmites in the caves. The
  ember veins, lava-veined basalt and glowing lichen are gone.
- Bright and misty: daylight scattered by haze, a little cooler deeper
  down, dimmer inside the caves and at night. Pale dust drifts in the
  updraft instead of embers. Airier music and wingbeats in the tube.
- Entrances you can find: three to five sinkholes per world, spread around
  the tube, each marked by a pale stone arch and a ring of standing stones
  visible from across the plain, and discovered from 75 m away.

SAVES
- Save format version 3: saves from 0.3.0 and earlier start their seed anew,
  because the Great Fault changed.

TESTS
- Creature suite: a travelling explorer keeps meeting creatures from the
  rim down into Layer 2, those left behind are folded back, and none linger
  outside the area.
- All 54 automated tests pass; the autopilot walks the reshaped cave network
  from the plain (861 m) to the Fourth Gate (1964 m) with real physics.
